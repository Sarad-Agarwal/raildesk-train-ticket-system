## **2. What is unique about PulseBoard AI?**

Five major differentiators:

1\. **Aspect-Level Sentiment Analysis:** One review can have multiple sentiments. Example: good food but poor delivery and damaged packaging.

2\. **AI Root Cause Discovery:** Explains why customers are unhappy, backed by actual customer reviews.

3\. **Early Warning System:** Detects increasing complaints before they become bigger business problems.

4\. **Smart Issue Prioritisation:** Scores problems from 0–100 based on complaint frequency, severity, and trends, helping businesses decide what to fix first.

5\. **Insight-to-Action:** Converts AI findings into practical business recommendations with To Do, In Progress, and Completed tracking.

### **Additional features**

- Time-based sentiment analysis with custom date ranges.
- Evidence-linked AI insights.
- AI-generated customer review responses.
- Automatic PDF and Excel business reports.
- Previous-period performance comparison.
