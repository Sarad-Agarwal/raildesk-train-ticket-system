PULSEBOARD AI Customer Feedback & Sentiment Intelligence Platform OpenAI
Hackathon – Problem Statement 6B

============================================================ 1. PROJECT
OVERVIEW ============================================================

PulseBoard AI is a browser-based Customer Feedback & Sentiment
Intelligence Platform.

Its purpose is to help business owners, brand managers and
administrators understand customer opinions over time, identify
recurring problems, monitor brand perception and make better business
decisions.

The central idea: Do not make businesses read hundreds of individual
reviews. Let AI analyse them, identify what changed, explain why
customers are unhappy and generate professional management reports.

The project extends the official 6B problem statement while retaining
its core requirements: - Browser-based interface. - Multi-line customer
feedback input. - AI classification into positive, neutral and negative
sentiment. - Brand health score. - Identification of recurring complaint
keywords. - Export of classified feedback as a spreadsheet.

Additional proposed capabilities: - CSV/Excel upload. - Time-based
sentiment monitoring. - Previous-period comparison. - Aspect-level
sentiment analysis. - AI-generated business observations and
recommendations. - Professional PDF reports. - Multi-sheet Excel
reports.

Important limitation: The MVP will analyse feedback supplied by the user
or included in its sample dataset. It will not claim to automatically
collect reviews from Google, Instagram or other platforms unless
authorised integrations are actually implemented.

============================================================ 2. TARGET
USERS ============================================================

1.  Business owner Wants to know whether customers are satisfied and
    what needs improvement.

2.  Marketing manager Wants to understand brand perception and recurring
    complaints.

3.  Administrator / management Wants periodic performance reports and
    evidence-based recommendations.

For the hackathon MVP, one admin-style dashboard is sufficient.
Complicated authentication and role management are not required
initially.

============================================================ 3. MODULE 1
– REVIEW DATA MANAGEMENT
============================================================

Purpose: Provide a simple way for a business to supply customer
feedback.

Input methods: - Upload a CSV file. - Upload an Excel file, if
implemented within the available time. - Paste multiple reviews. - Load
an included sample dataset. - Future possibility: connect authorised
review platforms through APIs.

Suggested review fields:

-   Review ID
-   Customer identifier or anonymised customer label
-   Review text
-   Rating
-   Source
-   Product or service
-   Location
-   Review date and time

Example:

Review ID: REV001 Customer: Customer 01 Review text: Delivery was very
slow. Rating: 2/5 Source: Website Product: Food Delivery Location:
Kolkata Date/time: 17 November 2026, 10:30 AM

Timestamps are essential for date-range filtering. The application
should validate uploaded columns and dates before analysis.

============================================================ 4. MODULE 2
– TIME-BASED SENTIMENT INTELLIGENCE
============================================================

This is a key product feature.

Suggested reporting filters: - Last 4 hours - Last 12 hours - Last 24
hours - Last 7 days - Last 15 days - Last 30 days - Last 2 months - Last
3 months - Last 6 months - Last 1 year - Custom date range

When the reporting period changes, all dashboard components should
update: - Total reviews - Positive, neutral and negative counts - Brand
health score - Complaint categories - Sentiment trends - AI-generated
insights - Exported reports

The application should use the timestamps actually present in the
dataset. It must not imply that it has live or historical platform
reviews that were never supplied.

Period-over-period comparison: Compare the selected period with an
equally sized previous period.

Example: - Previous week positive sentiment: 65% - Current week positive
sentiment: 72% - Change: +7 percentage points

Clearly distinguish percentage-point changes from percentage growth.

Date-filter definitions: - Last 4 hours: rolling four-hour window. -
Last 24 hours: rolling 24-hour window. - Last 7 days: rolling seven-day
window. - Last 30 days: rolling 30-day window. - Last 2 months: subtract
two calendar months from the current timestamp. - Custom: user-selected
start and end dates.

Display the actual date range and timezone used. For small sample sizes,
show a low-data warning.

============================================================ 5. MODULE 3
– AI SENTIMENT & ASPECT ANALYSIS
============================================================

The AI should not assign only one sentiment to an entire review. It
should identify the sentiment of individual aspects.

Example review: “The food was excellent, but the delivery was extremely
slow and the packaging was damaged.”

Expected analysis: - Food quality: Positive - Delivery: Negative -
Packaging: Negative

For each review, AI can extract: - Overall sentiment - Aspect or
category - Aspect-level sentiment - Emotion or dissatisfaction signal -
Complaint theme - Urgency - Short explanation

Possible feedback types: - Praise - Complaint - Suggestion - Feature
request - Service issue

Use structured AI output and validate the result before displaying or
storing it.

Illustrative structured response:

{ “overallSentiment”: “negative”, “aspects”: [ { “category”:
“food_quality”, “sentiment”: “positive” }, { “category”: “delivery”,
“sentiment”: “negative” } ], “issue”: “delivery_delay”, “urgency”:
“high”, “reason”: “Delivery was significantly delayed” }

============================================================ 6. MODULE 4
– BUSINESS HEALTH & ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
============================================================

The dashboard should include:

A. Overview cards - Total reviews - Positive reviews - Negative
reviews - Neutral reviews - Brand health score - Average customer rating

B. Sentiment distribution A donut chart showing positive, neutral and
negative feedback.

C. Sentiment over time A line chart showing how sentiment changes across
the selected period.

D. Complaint category analysis A bar chart showing the most frequently
reported issues.

E. Customer rating analysis Distribution of 1-star, 2-star, 3-star,
4-star and 5-star ratings.

F. Customer feedback table A searchable and filterable table
containing: - Original review - Date - Source - Rating - Sentiment -
Identified aspect or issue

All metrics and charts must use the same filtered dataset.

============================================================ 7. MODULE 5
– AI ROOT CAUSE OBSERVATIONS & RECOMMENDATIONS
============================================================

The application should move beyond reporting complaint percentages.

Example:

Observation: Delivery-related complaints account for a large proportion
of negative reviews.

Evidence: Customers repeatedly mention late deliveries, long waiting
periods and delayed order arrival.

Possible business impact: Repeated delivery problems may reduce customer
satisfaction and increase refund requests.

Recommended actions: 1. Investigate delivery performance during peak
hours. 2. Review delivery partner availability. 3. Monitor average
delivery times. 4. Check whether particular locations have recurring
delays.

Important: Distinguish facts observed in the dataset from AI-generated
hypotheses. Do not claim that a problem caused lost revenue unless the
dataset contains evidence for that claim.

============================================================ 8. MODULE 6
– PROFESSIONAL PDF & EXCEL REPORT GENERATOR
============================================================

The report generator is an important product feature.

The administrator selects a reporting period and exports the same
filtered analysis shown on the dashboard.

A. PDF report – proposed 6 to 8 pages

Page 1 – Cover - PulseBoard AI branding - Customer Voice & Sentiment
Report title - Reporting period - Prepared for - Generated date and time

Page 2 – Executive Summary - Total customer reviews - Brand health
score - Sentiment distribution - Key findings

Page 3 – Sentiment Analysis - Positive, neutral and negative

Page 4 – Customer Complaint Analysis - Top five complaints - Frequently
mentioned topics - Negative feedback analysis

Page 5 – Product & Service Performance - Aspect-wise sentiment -
Product/service strengths - Areas requiring improvement

Page 6 – AI Business Insights - Root cause observations - Emerging
customer concerns - Important positive feedback

Page 7 – Recommended Actions - High-priority actions - Medium-priority
actions - Suggested improvements

Page 8 – Appendix - Methodology - Review counts - Data sources -
Reporting limitations

PDF quality requirements: - Professional typography - Consistent
branding - Page numbers - Header and footer - Charts and KPI cards -
Reporting dates - Well-formatted tables - Generated timestamp

B. Excel report – one workbook with five worksheets

1.  Executive Summary KPIs, sentiment and key findings.

2.  Review Data All classified individual reviews.

3.  Sentiment Analysis Positive, neutral and negative statistics.

4.  Complaint Analysis Complaint categories and counts.

5.  Period Comparison Current versus previous period.

Excel quality requirements: - Formatted headers - Filters - Percentage
and date formats - Conditional formatting - Charts where practical

C. Export controls - Selected reporting period - Number of reviews
analysed - Option to include AI recommendations - Export Executive PDF -
Export Analytics Workbook

TECHNOLOGY STACK

Recommended stack for a fast, reliable hackathon prototype:

https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs -

============================================================ 10.
APPLICATION ARCHITECTURE
============================================================

Frontend: PulseBoard AI dashboard - Dashboard - Reviews - Filters -
Reports

Next.js API layer: - Upload validation - Analysis requests - Export data
preparation

OpenAI API: - Sentiment analysis - Aspect extraction - Complaint
themes - Business observations and recommendations

Data engine: - Review records - Timestamps - Analysis results - KPI
calculations - Date filtering

Report generators: - PDF generator - Excel generator

Data flow: 1. Administrator uploads a CSV. 2. Application validates
columns and timestamps. 3. Data engine prepares the reviews. 4. OpenAI
analyses each review. 5. Structured analysis results are associated with
original review IDs. 6. Results are cached or retained for reuse. 7.
User selects a date range. 8. Data engine filters results and calculates
KPIs. 9. Dashboard updates. 10. PDF and Excel generators use the same
filtered dataset.

Important architectural decision: AI analysis and date filtering must be
separate. Do not call the AI API again every time the user changes from
Last 7 Days to Last 30 Days. Analyse each review once, retain its result
and recalculate dashboard metrics from the retained results. This saves
API time and cost.

For the hackathon, browser memory or local storage can be used. A
production multi-user version should use persistent database storage.

============================================================ 11.
ANALYTICAL FORMULAS
============================================================

A. Sentiment distribution

Positive % = (Positive Reviews / Total Reviews) x 100

Neutral % = (Neutral Reviews / Total Reviews) x 100

Negative % = (Negative Reviews / Total Reviews) x 100

B. Brand Health Score

Proposed formula:

BHS = [Positive Reviews + 0.5 x Neutral Reviews] / Total Reviews x 100

This is a project-defined metric, not an official industry-standard
score. Explain its formula in the application/report.

C. Average rating

Average Rating = Sum of Ratings / Number of Rated Reviews

D. Complaint frequency

Complaint Rate = Reviews mentioning an issue / Total Reviews x 100

E. Period comparison

Change = Current Period Value - Previous Period Value

Example: Previous week positive sentiment: 65% Current week positive
sentiment: 72% Improvement: 7 percentage points.

Handle empty datasets and zero denominators safely.

============================================================ 15. JUDGE
DEMONSTRATION SCENARIO
============================================================

Scenario: A food delivery company wants to understand customer
satisfaction.

1.  Upload customer reviews. Load 500 synthetic reviews with different
    ratings, locations, sources and timestamps.

2.  Analyse customer feedback. AI processes reviews and identifies
    positive, neutral and negative sentiment.

3.  Show the last 7 days. Select the date filter and display sentiment
    distribution and complaint trends.

4.  Show the last 30 days. Demonstrate comparison with the previous
    period.

5.  Show AI findings. Highlight the major complaint categories in the
    sample dataset, such as delivery delays and packaging.

6.  Generate management reports. Download the professional PDF and Excel
    workbook.

The strongest demonstration moment: Show how the business can move from
hundreds of individual reviews to a prioritized list of business
problems and a shareable executive report.

============================================================ 16.
ALIGNMENT WITH OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT 6B
============================================================

Official requirements: - Clean browser-based web interface with
multi-line text input. - AI classification into positive, neutral or
negative sentiment. - Visual summary counters for brand health and
recurring complaint keywords. - One-click export of classified feedback
as a spreadsheet. - Suggested data: 30 diverse customer reviews and
social media comments.

PulseBoard AI retains all of these requirements and extends them with: -
CSV upload. - Time-based sentiment monitoring. - Historical period
comparison. - Aspect-level sentiment. - AI root cause observations. -
Actionable business recommendations. - Professional PDF reporting. -
Multi-sheet Excel reporting.

The product should remain focused on customer feedback and sentiment
intelligence.

============================================================ 17. FINAL
PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
============================================================

1.  Every dashboard number, chart and exported report must come from the
    same filtered review data.

2.  Analyse each review once and reuse the structured result. Do not
    repeatedly call AI when date filters change.

3.  Use timestamps that exist in the dataset. Do not claim access to
    live external reviews without real integrations.

4.  Clearly distinguish measured facts from AI-generated interpretations
    and recommendations.

5.  Show low-data warnings when the selected period contains too few
    reviews.

6.  Keep the API key on the server.

7.  Prioritize a complete, reliable MVP over optional features.
