# 1. PRODUCT VISION

## Product name

**PulseBoard AI**

## Tagline

"Understand your customers. Improve your business."

## One-line description

An AI-powered customer feedback intelligence platform that transforms thousands of customer reviews into actionable business decisions.

## The problem we are solving

Businesses receive customer feedback from multiple sources:

- Google reviews
- E-commerce reviews
- Food delivery platforms
- Customer surveys
- CSV and Excel files
- Customer support feedback
- Manually collected reviews

But collecting feedback is not the same as understanding it.

Business owners often face these problems:

1. They cannot quickly understand whether customer satisfaction is improving or declining.
2. They see an average rating but do not know what is causing negative feedback.
3. A single review may contain several positive and negative experiences.
4. They do not know which complaints are recurring.
5. They cannot easily identify emerging problems.
6. They do not know which business problem deserves immediate attention.
7. They lack practical recommendations based on actual customer feedback.
8. They spend hours manually reading reviews and preparing reports.
9. They cannot easily compare customer sentiment across products, locations, services, or time periods.

PulseBoard AI should solve these problems in one place.

## Our core promise

**Don't just read reviews. Discover what your customers are trying to tell you and what your business should do next.**

# 2. WHAT MAKES PULSEBOARD AI DIFFERENT?

This is extremely important.

Do not build another ordinary sentiment-analysis dashboard.

Our competitive advantage should be the combination of sentiment intelligence, root-cause discovery, issue prioritisation, trend detection, and actionable recommendations.

Implement the following differentiating features.

## Feature A: Aspect-Level Sentiment Intelligence

Ordinary sentiment analysis classifies an entire review as positive, neutral, or negative.

Our system must go deeper.

Example review:

"The food was delicious, but delivery was extremely late and the packaging was damaged."

Expected analysis:

- Food quality: Positive
- Delivery speed: Negative
- Packaging: Negative
- Overall sentiment: Negative or mixed, depending on the analysis
- Main complaint: Late delivery and damaged packaging
- Suggested urgency: High

One review can contain multiple aspects with different sentiment values.

Implement aspect-level analysis for relevant categories such as:

- Product quality
- Food quality
- Delivery speed
- Packaging
- Customer service
- Pricing
- Product availability
- Website experience
- Staff behaviour
- Payment experience
- Cleanliness
- Overall experience

Categories must be adaptable to the uploaded dataset.

Why this matters: Business owners can discover exactly which part of their service is creating dissatisfaction rather than receiving one generic negative score.

## Feature B: Root Cause Discovery

Do not simply display "Delivery is negative."

Explain what is happening.

Example:

"Delivery-related complaints increased during the selected period. Several customers mention delayed deliveries, particularly during evening hours."

Every AI insight should ideally contain:

- What happened
- Evidence from customer reviews
- Why it might be happening
- Business impact
- Suggested action
- Confidence level

Clearly distinguish actual evidence from possible explanations.

Never present an AI hypothesis as a verified fact.

## Feature C: Smart Issue Prioritisation

Create an intelligent issue-priority system.

Businesses usually have limited time and resources. They need to know what to fix first.

Each detected issue should receive a priority level:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

Consider:

- Number of affected reviews
- Negative sentiment percentage
- Recent increase in complaints
- Average rating associated with the issue
- Severity of the complaint
- Whether the problem is recurring

Create a transparent, explainable priority score from 0–100.

Display the factors contributing to that score.

Example:

**Delivery delays — Priority 91/100**

- 42 related complaints
- Negative feedback increased by 28%
- Several recent one-star reviews
- Repeated mentions of evening delivery delays

Suggested action: Review evening delivery capacity and delivery-partner allocation.

Do not claim the score represents a scientifically validated industry standard. It is a project-defined prioritisation model.

## Feature D: Early Warning System

This is one of the most important differentiators.

Detect when a negative issue starts increasing.

Example:

"Packaging complaints increased from 8% to 23% compared with the previous period."

Show:

- Previous-period complaint rate
- Current-period complaint rate
- Percentage-point change
- Number of affected reviews
- Whether the increase is significant enough to flag

Use clear labels such as:

- Emerging issue
- Worsening trend
- Improving issue
- Stable
- Insufficient data

Never manufacture an alert when there is no supporting data.

## Feature E: Time-Based Customer Intelligence

Provide flexible date filters:

- Last 4 hours
- Last 12 hours
- Last 24 hours
- Last 7 days
- Last 15 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 2 months
- Last 3 months
- Last 6 months
- Last 1 year
- Custom date range

All relevant dashboard metrics, charts, issue counts, AI insights, comparisons, and reports must respect the selected date range.

Also provide a comparison with the previous equivalent period when sufficient data exists.

For example:

Current 7 days vs previous 7 days.

Do not compare unrelated periods.

If the dataset does not contain enough records for a selected range, show an informative empty state rather than misleading results.

## Feature F: AI Business Action Plan

Convert insights into practical business recommendations.

Example:

Problem:
Customers are repeatedly reporting delayed deliveries.

Evidence:
38 negative reviews mention delivery delays.

Business impact:
Potential decline in repeat purchases and customer satisfaction.

Recommended actions:

1. Review delivery-partner performance.
2. Examine peak-hour delivery capacity.
3. Track delivery completion times.
4. Contact customers affected by severe delays.

Each recommendation should have:

- Priority
- Supporting evidence
- Expected business benefit
- Suggested next step

Where possible, allow users to mark recommendations as:

- To do
- In progress
- Completed

Store action status locally so it remains available after refreshing the page.

## Feature G: AI Review Response Assistant

Create an additional feature that generates professional responses to customer reviews.

Allow users to select a review and generate:

- Empathetic response
- Professional response
- Short response
- Apology-focused response
- Positive-review appreciation response

Responses must reference the actual review content.

Never invent promises, refunds, compensation, or actions that the business has not confirmed.

Allow users to copy the generated response.

## Feature H: Evidence-Linked Intelligence

This feature is essential for trust.

Every major AI insight should link back to the reviews that support it.

For example:

Insight: "Packaging complaints are increasing."

Provide an option to view the actual customer reviews that mention packaging.

The user must be able to verify the AI's interpretation.

Do not display unsupported AI-generated statistics.

# 7. AI ENGINE — IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Use the OpenAI API through a server-side Next.js route.

Suggested endpoint:

POST /api/analyze-reviews

Use environment variable:

OPENAI_API_KEY=

Provide an example environment file containing the variable name but no real secret.

The browser must never receive the secret key.

### AI analysis output

Use structured JSON output.

Each review should produce a predictable structure similar to:

{
  "overallSentiment": "positive | neutral | negative",
  "sentimentReason": "Short explanation",
  "aspects": [
    {
      "name": "Delivery",
      "sentiment": "negative",
      "evidence": "The delivery was extremely late",
      "confidence": 0.94
    }
  ],
  "mainIssue": "Late delivery",
  "urgency": "low | medium | high | critical",
  "suggestedResponse": "Professional response to the customer"
}

Validate all AI responses with Zod.

Handle malformed output and API failures gracefully.

### Batch processing

Do not make one API call per review if a batch approach is practical.

Implement batch processing with:

- Controlled batch sizes
- Progress indicators
- Error handling
- Retry strategy for transient failures
- Rate-limit awareness
- Ability to continue processing remaining batches

### Cache AI analysis

This is very important.

Analyse each review once and cache the result.

When users change date filters, do not repeat AI analysis for reviews that have already been analysed.

When new reviews are imported, analyse only the new reviews.

If the API is unavailable or the user has not configured an API key, provide a transparent demo mode.

Demo mode should use deterministic local analysis rules and clearly identify itself as demo analysis.

Never label rule-based demo results as genuine OpenAI-generated results.

### AI root cause insights

Use the structured review analysis to generate higher-level business observations.

For example:

- Delivery is the most frequently mentioned negative aspect.
- Packaging complaints have increased compared with the previous period.
- Customers frequently praise food quality.
- Customer support appears to be a weaker experience than product quality.

Insights must be supported by actual calculated data.

If an AI-generated observation includes statistics, those statistics must be calculated by the application and passed to the AI, not invented by the model.

### AI cost and performance

- Do not analyse the same review repeatedly.
- Do not call the API when users only change dashboard filters.
- Keep batch sizes manageable.
- Show analysis progress.
- Show which reviews are pending, completed, or failed.
- Make the system usable with a sample dataset even without an API key.
